# Audience and goals

## Primary visitor: founder or CEO

They need evidence that Frederic can create product direction, understand the business, build a capable team, work with technology, and ship through organisational complexity.

Desired action: start a conversation about a CPO, VP Product, Head of Product, advisory, or startup collaboration opportunity.

## Secondary visitor: CPO or CTO

They need evidence of product judgment, technical fluency, delivery leadership, team building, and constructive challenge.

Desired action: explore selected work, review the CV or LinkedIn profile, and make contact.

## Secondary visitor: executive recruiter

They need a fast, legible summary of scope, seniority, international experience, outcomes, and target roles.

Desired action: identify role fit and contact Frederic.

## Secondary visitor: peer, founder, or community organiser

They need to understand Frederic's builder mentality, AI practice, speaking interests, hackathons, mentoring, and startup support.

Desired action: propose a collaboration, talk, advisory engagement, or community project.

## Homepage comprehension goals

After the hero and first content section, visitors should understand:

- Frederic leads product and design.
- He is hands-on without being execution-only.
- His advantage is connecting disciplines.
- He has experience in regulated, technical, international products.
- He builds teams, systems, and products.

## Conversion goals

Primary:

- Email contact
- LinkedIn profile visit

Secondary:

- View selected case study
- Open CV
- Visit public GitHub repository after launch
- Explore builder work or recognition
