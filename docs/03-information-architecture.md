# Information architecture

## First-release model

A compact single-page homepage with links to future case-study pages.

## Homepage order

1. Centered introduction
   - Professional but relaxed portrait
   - Frederic Berghmans with a verified profile marker
   - Product and design leadership title
   - X, GitHub, and LinkedIn links
   - Quiet positioning statement
   - Primary contact action
   - Floating system, light, dark, and sound controls without a navigation bar

2. Selected impact
   - A small set of verified outcome signals
   - No metric wall

3. Selected work
   - AMINA digital banking platform
   - Building the product-design function
   - AI-assisted product delivery
   - International expansion

4. How Frederic operates
   - Product strategy
   - Design leadership
   - Technical and AI fluency
   - Regulated delivery

5. Beliefs
   - A compact selection of product beliefs

6. Builder and community proof
   - Hackathons
   - Startup support
   - Experiments
   - Speaking and mentoring

7. Experience and recognition
   - Compact career timeline
   - Selected recognition

8. Contact
   - Email
   - LinkedIn
   - CV
   - GitHub when public

9. Footer
   - Built by Fred with AI
   - Copyright
   - Theme and sound states if useful

## Navigation principle

The site should remain useful without a sticky multi-item navigation bar. On mobile, avoid a menu unless future pages make it necessary.

## Case-study model

Future case studies use a consistent structure:

1. Context
2. Problem
3. Frederic's mandate
4. Constraints
5. Decisions
6. Collaboration and delivery
7. Outcome
8. Reflection
9. Verified evidence
