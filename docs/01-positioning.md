# Positioning

## Primary positioning

Frederic is a product and design leader who turns complex business, regulatory, technical, and customer problems into products people want to use.

He moves between strategy, design, technology, AI, and delivery, and stays close enough to the work to build.

## Career direction

The portfolio should support applications and introductions for:

- Chief Product Officer at a startup or scale-up
- VP Product
- Head of Product
- VP Product Design
- Senior product and design leadership roles

The geographical scope is Switzerland, Europe, and international. Fintech and crypto are natural strengths, but the broader message is an ability to handle complexity.

## Differentiation

Frederic sits between:

- Business and customer value
- Product strategy and execution
- Design and frontend engineering
- Compliance, legal, and usable experiences
- Executive direction and delivery detail
- Traditional finance and crypto-native products
- Human judgment and AI-assisted workflows

He is not positioned primarily as a design engineer. Design-engineering ability is evidence of technical fluency and hands-on leadership.

## Leadership characteristics to demonstrate

- Questions assumptions and organisational habits
- Creates direction where little structure exists
- Builds teams and operating models
- Uses customer evidence to influence roadmaps
- Creates business cases for new products and tools
- Leads vendor selection and implementation
- Understands core systems and technical constraints deeply enough to improve product decisions
- Challenges scope and accepts pragmatic releases when required
- Drives measurable outcomes after delivery
- Connects stakeholders across product, engineering, compliance, legal, marketing, operations, and executives

## Beliefs

- Design should own product outcomes, not only interfaces.
- Delivery is not success unless it creates a measurable outcome.
- AI is collapsing the distance between design and engineering.
- Regulation is a design constraint, not an excuse for poor experiences.
- Senior leaders should remain close enough to the work to build.
- Product vision should define the desired experience before technical constraints reduce scope.
- Teams should learn from shipped products instead of treating launch as completion.

- Influenced technical and experience foundations including Expo, design tokens, localisation, accessibility, authentication, analytics, and design systems.
- Owns and shapes the digital customer experience roadmap with product leadership.
- Introduced tools including Intercom, PostHog, Crowdin, Lovable, and other product-development capabilities.
- Led product requirements and experience work supporting EU and UK expansion.
- Previously led designers and frontend developers at Bitcoin Suisse.
- Won a MetaMask design hackathon and contributed to civic technology projects.
