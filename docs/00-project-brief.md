# Project brief

## Product

A new personal portfolio for Frederic Berghmans.

## Purpose

The portfolio must reposition Frederic from a traditional product-design profile into a credible product and design leader who can move into CPO, VP Product, Head of Product, or VP Product Design roles.

It must show that he can operate across product strategy, customer experience, design, technology, AI, regulatory constraints, operating models, and delivery while remaining close enough to the work to build.

## Primary audiences

- Founders and CEOs hiring product leadership
- CPOs and CTOs hiring senior product or design leadership
- Executive recruiters
- Regulated fintech and crypto companies
- Complex technology companies outside financial services
- Startups seeking advisory or hands-on product leadership

## Initial release

The first release contains:

- Core visual system
- Responsive homepage
- Theme system with system, light, and dark modes
- Logo and name lockup
- Portrait treatment
- Homepage positioning
- Selected impact and work previews
- Product, design, AI, and builder capabilities
- Recognition and career snapshot
- Contact routes
- Optional sound control
- Analytics foundation

## Later releases

- Detailed case studies
- Builder experiments
- Occasional thoughts and articles
- Speaking and community work

## Non-goals for the first release

- CMS
- Regular publishing workflow
- Newsletter platform
- Complex 3D or WebGL
- Decorative glow
- Large multi-page site
- Exhaustive career archive
- Full recreation of confidential AMINA interfaces

## Success criteria

Within 60 seconds, a relevant visitor should understand that Frederic:

1. Is a product and design leader, not only a designer.
2. Turns complex business, regulatory, technical, and customer problems into products people want to use.
3. Moves between strategy and hands-on delivery.
4. Has meaningful AI and frontend fluency.
5. Can lead products and teams in complex international environments.

The site should generate relevant conversations about leadership roles, advisory work, startup collaboration, speaking, hackathons, mentoring, and selected consulting.
